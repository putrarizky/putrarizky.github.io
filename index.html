Optimizing Bank Loan Adoption via Cash-Flow Driven Underwriting

2026
35%DIR level where default risk spikes
3,818Pre-qualified leads identified

Background

The bank relied on traditional demographic targeting for marketing, resulting in a stagnant 15.16% Loan Adoption Rate. Leadership mandated aggressive growth, but pushing for volume with existing filters risked severely damaging the portfolio since the Non-Performing Loan rate was already at a critical 11.14%.

Objective

The primary objective was to increase the Loan Adoption Rate by 20%. Concurrently, the project aimed to implement a strict guardrail metric to cap the Non-Performing Loan rate securely below the standard 5% safety limit.

Method

Root-cause analysis, exploratory data analysis, and predictive modeling with Logistic Regression and Random Forest, followed by validation.

Result

Default probability spikes when a borrower's Debt-to-Inflow Ratio (DIR) exceeds 35%. I identified 3,818 leads for targeted in-app offers and recommended a hard 35% DIR underwriting guardrail, plus an early warning system for active loans reaching 50% DIR.

Revo Bank Customer Segmentation and Analysis

2026
Rp6.84BNet profit analyzed
33%DTI cap proposed for limit increases

Background

RevoBank is an Indonesian bank analyzing six months of client and transaction data. Operating on a thin 1.5% margin, the bank faces a 0.26% fraud rate where 66 successful transactions are required to recover a single fraudulent loss.

Objective

The objective is to increase credit card usage among existing customers by 10% over twelve months. The project aims to maximize profitability by understanding usage drivers, evaluating risk profiles, and creating user personas to enable targeted customer engagement.

Method

Data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, and customer clustering to build user personas.

Result

Net profit reached Rp6.84B against Rp1.20B in fraud losses. Active users carry a higher DTI (about 29%) than retired users (about 6%). I recommended VIP rewards for high-income clients, halting limit increases above 33% DTI, and installment plans for risky, active spenders.

Revo Hospital Data Visualization

2026
3,671Admissions analyzed
4 daysDischarge protocol recommended

Background

Revo Hospital faced operational bottlenecks, uneven bed turnover, and regional revenue concentration, affecting both clinical efficiency and financial sustainability.

Objective

Give the Head of Operations a dashboard to monitor bed utilization, Average Length of Stay (ALOS), and net revenue, supporting capacity planning and patient flow decisions.

Method

Cleaned and prepared transactional and demographic data, then built an interactive Tableau dashboard with branch, department and date filters.

Result

Revenue is concentrated in two Jakarta branches. Oncology (7.1 days) and Cardiology (6.2 days) show the longest stays, leading to recommendations for ward expansion, high-tech equipment investment in top branches, and a 4-day discharge protocol.
